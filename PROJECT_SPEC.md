# AMIT ESHTAR PORTFOLIO

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Create a modern premium portfolio website for Amit Eshtar.

The website represents a junior copywriter and creative.

The goal is to impress recruiters, creative directors and potential clients.

The website should feel like a premium creative portfolio rather than a corporate website.

The work should always be the hero.

Language:
Hebrew only.

Direction:
RTL.

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Minimal.

Editorial.

Clean.

Large typography.

Lots of whitespace.

Smooth animations.

Premium feeling.

No unnecessary decorations.

Use subtle transitions.

Every page should feel calm and elegant.

---

Do not invent text.

# PART 3 — HOME PAGE

## HERO SECTION

The Home page is the first impression of the portfolio.

Layout:

RTL.

Two-column layout.

RIGHT COLUMN:
Contains all text.

LEFT COLUMN:
Contains Amit's portrait.

Both columns are vertically centered.

The layout should feel clean, balanced and premium.

Do not place the portrait below the text.

---

## HERO CONTENT

Small label:

בוגר קורס קופירייטינג בהצטיינות

Main headline:

יש לי תיק עבודות.
יש לי גם מגירה מלאה ברעיונות
שעוד לא מצאו לקוח.

The headline should be very large and dominant.

Below the headline:

מחפשים את האתגר הבא.

Primary CTA button:

לכל העבודות

The button navigates to the Works page.

---

## PORTRAIT

Large portrait.

Rounded corners.

Object-fit: cover.

Keep the entire face visible.

No filters.

No grayscale.

---

## SCROLL ANIMATION

Hero fades in on load.

Portrait slides in softly.

Headline fades in with a slight delay.

Button fades in last.

Animations should remain subtle.

---

## BELOW THE HERO

Do not add any additional sections.

The Home page ends after the hero section.

The design should intentionally leave whitespace below.
